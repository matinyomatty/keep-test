About Keep

Keep is a point-of-sale and business app for shops and barbershops in Tanzania: sales, stock, WhatsApp receipts, cash-ups, debts and reports, in English and Kiswahili.

Messages we send

Keep sends WhatsApp receipts, debt reminders and account notifications on behalf of the shops that use it, only to those shops' own customers and staff. We do not send marketing messages, and a shop's customer data is never shared with other shops.
